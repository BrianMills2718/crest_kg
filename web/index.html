About CREST

From a source search to an evidence graph

This is an active research workbench. Search tracked CIA records and private uploads, ask an evidence-grounded question over a collection, inspect cited passages, then hand cited sources into the traced graph extraction pipeline.

Uploads use native PDF text when available and local Tesseract OCR for scans. Private documents and graphs require a tailnet identity or operator token. The live CIA connector remains explicit when official endpoints deny or redirect acquisition.

The five-document graph is a labeled example checkpoint, not a claim of corpus coverage.
